Features

Everything a modern transport platform needs

From booking a shared ride to running a full transport company - RideKSA does it all in one app.

Instant Booking

Search routes, pick your group and book a ride in seconds. Flat, transparent fares with no surprises.

Live Trip Tracking

Follow your ride on a live map with a foreground location service and real-time driver updates.

Captain Offers

Captains receive booking requests and make competitive offers. Passengers choose the best one.

Group Rides

Create a group ride, share an invite link and let friends join with a single tap - seat count respected.

Wallet Payments

Secure wallet with transaction history, invoices, commission tracking and penalty handling.

Auto Invoices

Every trip generates a professional invoice with items, letterhead and ZATCA-ready validation.

Ride Chat

Chat with your driver and co-passengers directly inside the app during the trip.

Company Transport

Contract bookings, employee transport, staff assignment and corporate agreements built in.

Routes & Places

Curated routes, saved places and autocomplete across Saudi cities - from Riyadh to Dammam.

Smart Notifications

Real-time alerts for offers, trip assignments, group joins and payments via FCM push.

Verified Captains

Driver documents, vehicle validation and approval flows keep every ride safe and compliant.

Admin Dashboard

Companies and admins get a full dashboard: fleets, drivers, earnings, commissions and subscriptions.

How it works

From request to ride in four steps

1

Search & book

Enter your route, pick a group or service type and get an instant quote with a fair, flat fare.

2

Get offers

Available captains view your booking and send offers. Compare and accept the best one.

3

Ride & track

Follow your trip live on the map, chat with your driver and enjoy a tracked, safe journey.

4

Rate & pay

Pay securely from your wallet, get an instant invoice and rate your captain when you arrive.

5

Bring friends

Share a group invite link and split the journey - each member joins with a single tap.

6

Run a company

Transport companies manage fleets, staff trips, contracts and commissions from one dashboard.
